Privacy Policy

Clearpen (a product of Louis IV Studio) · Last updated: October 2026

Clearpen helps you write clearly. This page explains what information we collect when you use clearpen.live or the Clearpen Android app, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in with Google or an email code, we receive your email address and basic profile details (such as your name) from the sign-in provider.
  • Usage and plan information. We record your plan (free or Pro) and how many refinements you have used, so we can apply free limits and unlock Pro features.
  • Payment information. Payments are handled by Paystack. We do not see or store your card details. We keep Paystack's reference codes for your customer and subscription so we can confirm your Pro status.
  • Text you submit for refinement. When you tap Refine, the selected text is sent to our server and passed to an AI service (Anthropic's Claude) to produce the improved version. Our server returns the result to you and does not save a copy of your text in our database.
  • Your documents. Documents you write are saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. We do not upload them to our servers except when you submit text for refinement as described above. Clearing your browser or app data removes them.
  • Technical and analytics information. We use Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity and Vercel Analytics to understand how the site is used, and Sentry to detect errors. These tools may collect information such as your device type, browser, approximate location, pages visited and interactions with the site.

2. How we use information

  • To provide and operate Clearpen, including AI refinement, sign-in, and PDF export.
  • To apply usage limits, process upgrades, and confirm your plan.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse, and fix problems.
  • To understand usage and improve the product.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Who we share it with

We use trusted service providers who process information on our behalf:

  • Supabase — account sign-in and database
  • Anthropic — AI processing of text you submit for refinement (subject to Anthropic's own data policies)
  • Paystack — payments
  • Vercel — website hosting and analytics
  • Google — sign-in and Google Analytics
  • Microsoft — Clarity analytics
  • Sentry — error monitoring

We may also disclose information if required by law.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your account and plan information for as long as your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we will delete it, except for records we must keep for legal or payment reasons.

5. Your choices and rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete the information we hold about you, and you can stop using Clearpen at any time. Depending on where you live, laws such as Nigeria's Data Protection Act 2023 may give you additional rights.

6. Deleting your account

To delete your account and the data linked to it, email us at the contact address below from the email you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my account". We will confirm once it is done.

7. Children

Clearpen is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

8. Security

We use reputable providers and encrypted connections (HTTPS). No online service is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.
