Privacy Policy

Clearpen, operated by Louis IV Studio (Nigeria) · Last updated: 27 October 2026

In plain language. Clearpen helps you improve your writing. You can use it without an account. If you sign in, we keep your email, plan and usage count. When you tap Refine, the text you selected is sent through our server to an AI service (Anthropic's Claude) and the result comes back to you. We do not save that text in our database. Your documents are stored in your own browser, not on our servers. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads. You can ask us to delete your account at any time (see section 9).

This policy applies to the website clearpen.live and the Clearpen Android app, which displays the same website. It explains what information we handle, why, who else handles it, and your choices.

1. Who we are

Clearpen is a product of Louis IV Studio, based in Nigeria. We decide how and why the information described here is used (we are the "controller"). Contact: clearpenapp@gmail.com.

2. Information we handle

Information you give us

  • Account details. If you sign in with Google or an email code, we receive your email address, your name if your Google account provides it, and a user ID.
  • Text you submit for refinement. The text you select and send, and the writing mode you choose (for example Professional or Legal). It is processed to produce your result and is not saved in our database. Please do not enter passwords, ID or card numbers, health records, or other highly sensitive information.
  • Messages and waitlist sign-ups. If you use our contact or waitlist form, we receive the name, email and message you type.
  • Payment details. Paying for Pro happens on Paystack's checkout. We never see or store your card number. We keep Paystack's customer and subscription reference codes and the date you subscribed, to confirm your plan.

Information created as you use Clearpen

  • Plan and usage. Your plan (free or Pro) and how many refinements you have used, so we can apply free limits and unlock Pro.
  • IP address. We use your IP address briefly on our server to apply free-use limits to signed-out visitors and to stop abuse. Our hosting provider also keeps standard technical logs (such as time, page and IP address).
  • Technical and usage data. Device and browser type, approximate location from your IP address, pages visited, interactions, and error reports. Section 5 lists the tools that collect this.

Stored on your device

  • The document you are writing and some interface settings are kept in your browser's local storage. We cannot see them. Clearing your browser or app data removes them.
  • If you sign in, your sign-in session is also kept in your browser's storage.
  • Files you open in the editor, such as Word documents, are read in your browser.

3. How we use information, and why

  • To provide Clearpen: sign-in, AI refinement, export, and Pro features (performance of our service to you).
  • To apply limits, process upgrades, and prevent abuse and fraud (our legitimate interests, and legal obligations).
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.
  • To understand how the service is used and improve it (our legitimate interests, or your consent where the law requires it).
  • To reply to messages you send us.

We do not sell your personal information, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not use your text to train any AI model.

4. Who we share information with

We use service providers who process information for us. Each also has its own privacy policy.

  • Anthropic — AI processing of the text you submit for refinement. Anthropic handles it under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Supabase — account sign-in and our database (email, plan, usage, payment reference codes).
  • Paystack — payments (including card and Google Pay options on its checkout).
  • Vercel — website hosting, server functions and Vercel Analytics.
  • Google — Google sign-in, Google Analytics and the Google Play Store.
  • Microsoft — Microsoft Clarity analytics.
  • Sentry — error monitoring.
  • Formspree — delivering contact and waitlist form messages to us.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights, our users or the service.

5. Analytics and similar technologies

We use Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity and Vercel Analytics to understand how the site is used. Google Analytics and Clarity may set cookies or similar identifiers and may record usage such as pages viewed, clicks, scrolling and the content shown on screen. Sentry records technical error information. You can block or clear cookies in your browser settings, though parts of the service may work differently.

6. International transfers

Our providers operate in several countries, so your information may be processed outside Nigeria, including in the United States and Europe. We choose established providers and rely on their safeguards for these transfers.

7. How long we keep information

  • Account and plan information: while your account is active, and until deletion is completed after you ask.
  • Text submitted for refinement: not stored by us. Anthropic's own retention rules apply to its processing.
  • Payment references and records: as long as we need them for accounting, disputes or legal reasons. Paystack keeps its own records under its policy.
  • Analytics and logs: kept for the periods set by each provider.

8. Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of the information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to some uses, or move it to another service. You can withdraw consent where we rely on it. Under Nigeria's Data Protection Act 2023 you may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. Depending on where you live, other laws may give you further rights. To use any right, email clearpenapp@gmail.com. We may need to confirm it is really you before acting.

9. Delete your account

To delete your Clearpen account and the data linked to it, email clearpenapp@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Clearpen account". We will confirm and complete deletion within 30 days. We may keep limited records that the law or accounting requires, such as proof of a payment. Cancelling a Pro subscription is done with Paystack, so please tell us if you also want us to help with that. Documents stored in your browser can be removed by clearing site or app data.

10. Children

Clearpen is meant for people aged 13 and over. If you are under 18, please use it with a parent or guardian's permission. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you think a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

11. Security

We use reputable providers and encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to our database is restricted. No online service is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security. Please protect your sign-in and tell us if you suspect misuse of your account.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. If we make a significant change, we will show a notice on the site before it takes effect.

13. Contact

Questions, requests or complaints: clearpenapp@gmail.com
