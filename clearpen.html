What We Collect

Your documents are stored in your browser's local storage — they never leave your device unless you export them. If you sign in, we store your email and subscription status to enable Pro features across devices. Waitlist data is handled by Formspree.

What We Don't Collect

  • We do not store your documents on our servers.
  • We do not read or analyse your writing content.
  • We do not sell your data.

AI Refinement

Only the selected text is sent to our server and forwarded to Anthropic's Claude API. Text is processed in real-time and not stored by Clearpen.

Frequently asked

Questions answered.
Doubts cleared.

Does Clearpen read or store my documents?+

No. Your documents are stored only in your browser's local storage — they never leave your device unless you export them. When you use AI Tone Refinement, only the selected text is sent for processing, and it is not stored by Clearpen.

Can I save as PDF without downloading any app?+

Yes — that's one of the core reasons Clearpen exists. Tap Export → Print / Save as PDF. Your browser opens a print dialog. Select "Save as PDF" as the destination. Your document downloads as a properly formatted A4 PDF. No app, no Adobe, no account required.

What is the difference between the tone modes?+

Professional removes informal language and hedging. Assertive makes you sound confident without apology. Legal adds formal, official-sounding phrasing for notices and complaints. Calmer softens aggressive language. Simpler removes jargon. Academic brings scholarly register for essays.

What happens after I use my 5 free refinements?+

You can still write, format, use templates, upload files, and export to PDF — all for free. The 5-use limit applies only to AI Tone Refinement. Upgrade to Pro (₦5,000/month) for unlimited refinements.

Does it work on my phone?+

Yes. Clearpen is built mobile-first. It works on Android Chrome, iOS Safari, and all major mobile browsers. The editor, tone analysis, AI refinement, templates, and file upload all work on mobile through the floating buttons and bottom sheet panel.

Can I upload my existing documents?+

Yes. Upload .txt, .docx (Word), and .pdf files directly into the editor. Your file is read locally in your browser — nothing is sent to a server during the upload process.

Which AI model powers Clearpen?+

Clearpen uses Claude by Anthropic. Your API requests are processed securely through Clearpen's server and never expose your data beyond Anthropic's standard processing.

How does Pro payment work?+

Pro is ₦5,000/month, paid securely via Paystack. You can pay with card, bank transfer, or USSD. Your account upgrades immediately after payment. Cancel anytime by emailing us.
