The core insight

Your grammar is fine.
Your tone isn't.

Grammarly checks spelling. Clearpen reads the room. One click changes how you sound — without changing what you mean.

Choose refinement mode:

Before
Hey, I was just wondering if maybe we could possibly discuss my performance review? I don't want to be a bother but it's been a while...
After — Professional
I would like to schedule a performance review discussion at your earliest convenience. It has been three months since our last evaluation meeting.
